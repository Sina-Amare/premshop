# ADR-0028 — A product page is facts in fields and prose in sections of six kinds; its questions live in `catalog`

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-10-20

**Context.** Until now a product page had a one-line summary, one plain-text description, the spec rows and the plans. That cannot carry what the first two real pages need: ChatGPT Plus needs four kinds of prose (what it does for whom, what is included, what happens after payment, the conditions before buying), a gift card needs two, and neither should be forced into the other's order. Some of what a page says is a commitment: delivery, warranty, refunds, the provider's stance on Iran. A competitor's page was seen stating one product's warranty three ways in three places, and a page this shop prints must not be able to do that. Much of the rest goes stale in weeks: model names, quotas, which countries a feature reaches. One operator writes and publishes all of it, and the policies some sentences depend on are still undecided, so a page has to be able to wait for them without being published by mistake. The earlier data-model draft also put a product's FAQ in `cms`, which may not import any domain app (services-and-modules §1).

**Decision.**

1. **Facts are fields, printed only by the template.** Prose never repeats them, so two parts of a page cannot disagree. On `Product`: `iran_stance` (the provider's official stance in its own words; «موضوعیت ندارد» is a valid value, and the page then says nothing about Iran), `refund_note` (one sentence, only where this product differs from the shop's refund rule), `sources` (the official pages behind the claims, one address per line, never printed), `facts_checked_on` and `time_sensitive`. The warranty stays the existing choice field; its conditions belong with the shop's other promises once the owner has decided them. On `Plan`: `price_confirmed_at`, set by any change to a price and by the panel's «قیمت‌ها را امروز بررسی کردم»; a page prints the oldest confirmation among the plans it shows, never the newest, and no date at all while one of them has none.
2. **Prose is `ProductSection` rows of six fixed kinds**: «مزیت‌ها و کاربردها», «چه چیزهایی می‌گیرید», «مقایسه», «بعد از پرداخت», «پیش از خرید», «متن آزاد». Each has an optional title (the kind's name by default, none for free text), a body in the restricted Markdown of ADR-0027, an optional image that requires its alt text, and an order the operator drags. The kind decides the template, `templates/catalog/sections/<kind>.html`; there is no free layout, no raw HTML, no nesting and no styling per section. A section copied from another product is a row of its own, and is copied only onto a product that is not live: a copy is written straight to the page, past the publishing rule, and a draft's sections carry the placeholders it waits on. A section added on the form goes after the product's last one.
3. **«پیش از خرید» is printed on every product page, in a fixed place,** whether or not the product has such a row: the warranty, the refund exception and the stance on Iran come first from their fields, then the operator's text. A product has at most one such row, held by a partial unique constraint, so a condition of sale is said once.
4. **A product's questions are `catalog.ProductQuestion`** (the buyer's question, an answer in restricted Markdown, an order), not a `cms` table: a question about a product belongs to the product, and `cms` must not depend on `catalog`. `cms` keeps the site-wide pages (S11). The product page lists the product's questions before the shop's own delivery and password questions.
5. **One publishing rule, `Product.publish_problems()`,** read by the form's save, the list's bulk action and the readiness list at the top of a draft's form. A product is not published while its delivery type, an available plan, its one-line summary or its stance on Iran is missing, or while that stance is anything but «موضوعیت ندارد» and `sources` is empty; while any text the page prints — its own fields, its specs, plans, sections or questions — holds an owner placeholder (`[تصمیم مالک…]`, `[وابسته به تصمیم…]`, `[از تحویل آزمایشی…]`); or, for a time-sensitive product, while its facts were last checked more than seven days ago. A product already live is held to the placeholders only, so a fact going stale does not lock every other edit to it. Each row the page prints asks this of itself in `clean()`, so any form that saves one asks it, the plan's own form in the panel included; on the product form, the rows of an inline the operator may not change are read as stored before the product goes live.
6. **A draft opens at its own address for staff who may view products,** under a «پیش‌نویس» band, with `noindex` and `Cache-Control: private, no-store`, because the same address will be the public page. For everyone else it is a 404, and an unavailable product is a 404 for everyone. This is the one exception to `Product.objects.public()`.
7. **Uploaded images are redrawn before they are stored.** A logo or a section image is accepted as PNG, JPEG or WebP judged by what Pillow reads, not by its name; it is redrawn to fit 512 pixels for a logo and 1600 for a section image, with its metadata left behind (a photo's location among it), and stored under a random name. SVG is refused, since it can carry script; the logos already stored as SVG stay until replaced.

**Alternatives considered.**

- **A fixed set of text fields in a fixed order.** No place for benefits or a comparison, and ChatGPT Plus and a gift card would read in the same order with half the gift card's fields empty.
- **A page builder, free blocks or a JSON document.** Layout chosen per product is design done per product, and no template could then promise that the conditions of sale are on the page.
- **The stance on Iran as a `ProductSpec` row titled «ایران».** The rule would have to find the row by its title in both spellings of ی, and a mistyped title would silently drop the requirement. A field the rule can require cannot be mistitled.
- **A `ProductSource` table holding a supporting quote per page, re-read by a script that alone sets `facts_checked_on`.** The right shape once the re-read exists; until it does, the table would hold quotes nothing reads. The addresses are kept as lines and the date is typed by the person who checked.
- **The product's questions in `cms.FAQ`.** `cms` would have to import `catalog`, against the app order.

**Consequences.**

- `Product.full_description` and `Category.intro_html` are off every form and page. Catalog 0020 moved a full description into a «متن آزاد» section wherever the page used to print it (where it differed from the summary) and a category's HTML introduction into the Markdown `Category.intro`; the columns stay until that migration no longer needs reversing, and are dropped by a later one.
- `facts_checked_on` records that a person checked, not that the sources still say what the page says.
- The rule reads only the product's own rows. It cannot see a placeholder in the shop-wide promises, or tell whether the warranty choice reflects a decided policy; a page whose warranty terms wait on the owner carries that placeholder in its «پیش از خرید» text.
- Markdown outside the subset is not refused when it is saved (ADR-0027); it reaches the page as typed.
- Every plan saved before this has no `price_confirmed_at`, so no page shows a price date until the operator confirms its prices.

**Assumptions.** One operator, entering a few dozen products. The sections' text is short enough to render on every request. The owner's undecided policies are written into the text as placeholders rather than guessed.

**Revisit when.** The source re-read script exists: `ProductSource` arrives with it and `facts_checked_on` is set only by a re-read that accepts every source. Three products need something none of the six kinds covers: a seventh kind with its own template, never a free block. A second person edits content: versions and an approval step. The same text is kept identical on five or more products and an edit is missed on one of the copies: shared text linked from each product.
