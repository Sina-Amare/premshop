# ADR-0016 — Brand & type: Estedad display over Vazirmatn body; teal on warm stone

**Status:** Accepted (owner-selected Option A) · 2026-09-01

**Context:** The visual base is a conversion input, not decoration — the shop's obstacle is customer doubt, and "one neutral family for everything" is exactly the generated-template signature the working agreement bans.

**Decision:** Body **Vazirmatn** (OFL) 400/500/700; headings **Estedad** (OFL-1.1, verified from the primary repo) 600/800 — contrast through skeleton and weight, not costume. Accent **teal-700 `#0F766E`** on a warm stone neutral ramp; **amber `#D97706` reserved exclusively for time meaning** (SLA badges, payment windows); green/red for success/danger, always paired with text. Persian digits in prices/dates, Latin in inputs; no letter-spacing on Persian, no italics, no uppercase. Fonts self-hosted only. Full token sheet and scale: [../design-language.md](../design-language.md).

**Alternatives considered:** Markazi Text headings (OFL naskh-serif — warmer editorial voice; runner-up, rejected for text-face limits at hero sizes and a weaker Latin companion); Lalezar (single-weight poster voice); commercial faces (paid web licenses unjustified against Estedad); unverifiable-license candidates excluded on principle.

**Consequences:** The palette is small on purpose — if a new color "is needed," the design is drifting; and if amber ever means anything but time, the operator's eye loses its one trained signal.

**Amendment — 2026-10-13 (owner's review).** The palette's *structure* changes, its meanings do not. The canvas becomes white with a neutral ramp instead of warm stone; one 4px radius replaces the two-radius set; headings drop from 800 to 600 and the scale shrinks; a hairline rule under a section head replaces boxing. Teal remains the single action colour and amber remains time-only — both decisions above stand, and the accent budget is unchanged. What is new is where colour comes from: product brand marks now render in their own colours, so the shelf carries the chroma and the interface stays quiet. Rationale and the full clause list: `docs/design-language.md`, "Amendment — 2026-10-13".

**Amendment — 2026-10-14 (owner's second review).** The meanings in this ADR still hold and none of them moved: teal is still the single action colour, amber still means time and only time, the faces are still Estedad over Vazirmatn, self-hosted. What changes is that the system now has somewhere dark to stand. Ink becomes a *surface* as well as a text colour (`--color-deep`, with its own text ramp), so the page opens and closes on a band of it; the single-radius clause from 2026-10-13 becomes two radii that encode control-versus-container; and exactly one shadow token enters the stylesheet with exactly one consumer. The accent budget is unchanged, and the primary action on an ink surface is paper rather than a brightened teal — raising teal's lightness until it passed contrast on near-black would have produced the acid-accent-on-black that this ADR's whole premise bans. Reasoning, alternatives and the revisit condition: [ADR-0026](0026-storefront-composition.md).

**Amendment — 2026-10-20 (the palette deepens).** The meanings above still hold and none of them moved: turquoise is the one colour for what a person does and what they chose, amber means time and nothing else, and the faces are Estedad over Vazirmatn, self-hosted. What changes is the palette under them, taken from Iranian tilework so that the approved teal family deepens instead of giving way to a new hue.

- **The accent darkens** from `#0F766E` to `#0A6A6E`, so white text on it rises from 5.47:1 to 6.36:1, with `#085A5E` and `#064A4E` for hover and press and `#E3F0EE` behind a chosen plan. It marks the primary action, the chosen plan and the current category, at most three times in a view, never on a heading or an icon.
- **The canvas returns to warm paper**, the stance this ADR began with: `#F6F4EF` under the page, and white only for the sheets the shop issues (the rate sheet, the پیش‌فاکتور, forms). The neutral ramp is warm again, from `#1C1A17` ink to `#655F56` muted, and a new `--color-control` (`#857D70`) gives the edge of anything a person operates at least 3:1.
- **Lapis (`#233D86`) is new and means the record:** the band over a document the shop issues, the double rule under the amount paid, text links, the focus ring and the lines of the tile art. It is never a broad background, a button or a fill, which keeps it from reading as a bank's colour, and a lapis focus ring is never mistaken for a turquoise choice.
- **Glaze (`#34A7A9`) is new and lives only in the illustrations.** At 2.64:1 on paper it can carry neither text nor an edge.
- **Amber is unchanged** (`#D97706`, text `#92400E`). The illustrations carry no yellow or ochre, so nothing on a page comes near it. Green and red for status are unchanged, except red's tint: mixed into the warm paper like the others, it came out 3/255 from the time tint and an error read as a deadline, so it is a cool pink set by hand (`#F9E1E1`, red text 6.68:1 on it).
- **Ink stays a surface** for the top strip and the footer (`#171614`); the ink hero was withdrawn on 2026-10-14b and does not return.

The consequence above holds with one gloss: two colours were added on purpose, each with one job, and a third would still be drift.
